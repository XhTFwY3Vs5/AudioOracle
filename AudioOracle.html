First time? Read this.
  • Standard — draws a phrase from your list. Weighted syntax: [3] yes makes "yes" 3× as likely.
  • Mythic Fate — rolls the Mythic Fate Check (2d10 + odds + chaos). Choose Odds and a Chaos Factor.
  • Mythic Meaning — rolls Action word pairs for inspiration. Add descriptors for two more words.
  • Append meaning words — in Standard or Fate, adds an Action pair to every roll.
  • Keyboard: Space starts/stops. R rolls once. Click a history entry to re-hear it.
  • On phones, the voice list may take a moment to populate. Grant the page screen-wake permission for long sessions.

    Weighted phrase syntax
    • [3] yes — "yes" is 3× as likely.
    • maybe — unweighted lines count as weight 1.
    • [0.5] perhaps — fractions allowed.
    • [0] never — weight 0 = kept in list but never drawn.
    • Malformed lines (e.g. [abc] foo) are treated as literal text at weight 1.
